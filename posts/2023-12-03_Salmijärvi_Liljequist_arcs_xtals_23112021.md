### 4/6 - 2023-12-03 19:25 UTC

2023 Nov 20/21 - stage 3 - 4/6. More crystals. I found an interesting discussion on crystal sampling in the comments of this blog post: https://t.co/eXQBiAMxcF

The answer is that, save for perhaps the most wildly changing displays, well organized one guy can do it all, photograph the halos and have crystal samples targeted to different stages of the display. I have also devised a simple protection to avoid sample pollution during photography. It is a cardboard plate with a hole for microscope objective. You can completely seal the dish this way. That's at least the theory, still waiting for the field trial.

### 5/6 - 2023-12-03 19:58 UTC

2023 Nov 20/21 - stage 3 - 5/6. More crystals. But are we now any the wiser on how these subparhelic arcs form? And why only the outer arcs? Seeking for that answer from the crystal sample is compounded by the fact that the display has also parhelia, subparhelia and parhelic circle, meaning not all of those crystals caused the subparhelic arcs, maybe just a minor proportion. So let's wait for that display that has only the subparhelic arcs. It is not fantasy, such a display has been photographed: https://t.co/89BDgSwQD9

And one more thing. The presence of parhelic circle makes the absence of 46 degree halos even more strange.
