### 1/9 - 2026-02-07 22:19 UTC

7/8 Feb 2026, Rovaniemi. The theme of the night was Moilanen arc. Took also crystal samples. Here is a single exposure from the beginning when Moila was at its best. Actually, I think it was the best I have seen. Typically, in the beam Moila is not conspicuous visually – you can't see any shape in it even if it the shape comes out fine in a single image. But this one was a clear, solid V-shape also visually. Dynamic range of the display was challenging, though. Had leave anything outside 22° halo pretty dark in order not to overexpose Moila.

The crystals were small, so I don't expect any breakthroughs. But it seemed like I have made some progress in fixing the milky central area that has plagued my photos. I took off the condenser and judging by what I saw from the camera screen, the photos came out better.

This was from the backyard gunning in Rautiosaari area. I had to photograph in a small field surrounded by trees. The car showed -19 C on arrival, upon leaving -25 C. The display was still going when I left, but not nearly as good as in the beginning. HID first, then Olight Javelot Turbo.

### 4/9 - 2026-02-08 10:34 UTC

7/8 Feb 2026, Rovaniemi. Spotlight comparison for what it's worth. At one point I changed the HID (above) to Olight Javelot Turbo (below), not touching the camera settings. Comparison is hampered by the display seeming to have weakened during the change. Also the Olight beam does not seem to be as well centered as the HID beam.

Visually I prefer to be bathed in HID beam for its better immersion. The benefit of Olight is the smooth non-artifact beam. And of course you don't need to haul along a car battery, so it is unbeatable when you for example want to have the light on top of a steep mound.

### 6/9 - 2026-02-08 11:58 UTC

7/8 Feb 2026, Rovaniemi. The microscope lighting issue indeed seems now to have been fixed, but something has been gnawing at the crystals. This particular ignition fuel I use has its contents stated and they are all hydrocarbons, so nothing water soluble there. So it's the lamp then.
