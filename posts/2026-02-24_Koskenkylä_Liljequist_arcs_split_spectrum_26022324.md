### 1/8 - 2026-02-24 16:57 UTC

23/24 Feb 2026, Rovaniemi. Left around 7 pm but had to wait for the ice fog to take over. Before that, there was only intermittent action in the vicinity of the gun, as shown here. This time, Liljequist arcs exhibit the "split spectrum" https://t.co/W7arGkb2MG, which is one of the many mysterious aspects of these arcs.

Once ice fog took over, it happened at the turn of a switch – suddenly large areas towards east, that had just a moment ago been completely clear, were enshrouded. I took photos and crystals in one location, but the display was really poor. Went further east to the Jokkavaara gravel pits, however, the action had not yet reached there. Waited for a while, but felt worn out and started towards the city and the apartment. Of course I should have persevered: as I drove back, the ice fog  had crept further east and was just around the corner. Car -25 C.

At the apartment, around 3 am I noticed an elevated, transparent fog layer had formed (a very common feature this winter). At sunrise it was largely cloudy, but started clearing soon and after 10 am I left for a round. Nothing to see – the gun had again fallen silent.

I believe the gunning will continue well into March, but looks like the coming night will be the last night for some days because it is getting windy and warming up. For the longest time (that's how I feel at least) we are going to have above freezing temps.
