### 1/1 - 2024-02-08 06:46 UTC

I hauled the gear back to apartment and returned out to move the car to the parking slot. But during those couple of minutes I was in, the action had started properly. Pillars all around. No choice but haul the gear back to car and get going. What it got then was the display in this video. It just went on and on. The display wouldn't stop, but I thought I had worked on it enough and returned back to apartment at 0630. The temp was -35 C when I left but it felt oddly warm. I think I read it wrong and it was -25 C. This would be in line with Apukka station, which always has pretty much the same temps that I get.
