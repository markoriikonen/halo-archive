### 1/6 - 2026-01-26 19:20 UTC

26/27 Jan 2026, Rovaniemi. Another windscreen elliptical halo! This time I didn't see it when I first went to check the situation. Only the poorly defined colored arc below the light (Parry?) and was having an internal dialogue whether it is worth documenting.

Came back with phone and Canon, placed the floody headlamp on top of the seat backrest, and then, from the Canon live view, noticed the ellipse. Two ellipses actually, far apart from each other.

These must be common – we just haven't had the eye to see. But this one, unlike the earlier ellipse, was hard to make out with the naked eye even when I knew it was there.

And oh, the season continues after all. Juujärvi racing has started making snow near Sierijärvi.
