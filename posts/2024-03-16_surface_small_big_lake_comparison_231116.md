### 1/10 - 2024-03-16 13:03 UTC

Prefer small lakes for surface displays. From 16 November 2023 in Rovaniemi, in this thread are stacks right after sunrise from Norvajärvi, which is the large lake, and then from Norvalampi, which is its tiny eastern end separated by narrow isthmus (järvi and lampi mean both lakes, the latter is used when it is relatively small). The result is typical, the display being much better in Norvalampi, the stacks of which are shown downstream. Displays on big lakes are spoiled by too much wind. Also, it does not help that on big lakes the surface is more uneven due to drift-snow. Norvajärvi stacks have 254, Norvalampi 251 frames.

### 2/10 - 2024-03-16 13:24 UTC

16 November 2023 in Rovaniemi, Norvalampi. Although the max stack has hyperrealed the display, it is faithful to the visual what comes to the individual halos that I could see: 9, 22 and 46° halos and a ghostly suggestion of 24° halo. That's how the max stacks practically always are, snowing no more or less halos than what was possible to see with the eye. Noise-to-signal ratio does not increase them as in average stacks, so they don't probe deeper into the display.

### 3/10 - 2024-03-16 13:50 UTC

16 November 2023 in Rovaniemi. For reference a single photo of the Norvalampi display. The map shows the locations, 1 for Norvajärvi, 2 for Norvalampi (which is unnamed in this map), 4 km apart. I will add downstream in coming days crystal photos from the latter display.  

The isthmus cutting off Norvalampi has a small parking shoulder and tourism companies bring people here to photograph auroras. They are always led by the guides on the Norvajärvi side, but every time I arrive to the location I am kind of holding my breath in fear of seeing "my" lake trampled. Well, in the end it got spoiled anyway as snow mobile tracks gradually spreaded all over.
