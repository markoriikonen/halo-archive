### 1/4 - 2024-04-19 16:29 UTC

I exhausted all puddles from two snow dumps this morning (19 April 2024, Rovaniemi) and got heaps of Ujvarosi stars. Here is one of them at the old snow dump by the bog Isoaapa (actually, not "old" anymore for they have just opened it after being closed all winter - they are still removing the snows even if much has melted already). The interesting thing is the second colored parhelion on the right pointing spike, best at about 00:10-00:25. The options are 46° stuff through 90 degree prism, rotated parhelion (from three prism face hit raypaths) and parhelion of parhelion. The outer parhelion colors are not any deeper from the inner one so I would be inclined to count out the first option. Ujvarosi has also similar feature in her standard setting observation (the second last image of four photos): https://t.co/TSOowbjoMl

At first the plate topside is facing camera, at 01:16 I turn it around. From 01:57 the bottomside is up
