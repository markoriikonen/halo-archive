### 1/8 - 2025-01-04 13:11 UTC

4 January 2024 Rovaniemi. I have been wondering about the lack of ice fog. There has been – in the last two or three days – only water fogs, save for some random ephemeral pockets of ice fog. I thought could it be somehow that gunning makes water fog because Ounasvaara seemed to be constantly in fog while around it was clearer. But today when the skies cleared it turned out that they are running guns without pressure air as evident from lack of any cloud (first image). Compare that to the hale and hearty Suosiola powerplant plume (three other images) which made a little display as viewed here from the top of the asphalt pile in Anttilanvaara. Ounasvaara is seen right behind that power plant, nothing coming from there. 

I had considered the possibility of no pressure air, but didn't quite want to believe it because there was some cloud from guns. Probably that was because of the foggy conditions.

So it is for the Suosiola plant to deliver the goods tonight. Apukka is already at -30 C, so odd radii could be on the cards.

### 2/8 - 2025-01-04 13:19 UTC

4 January 2024 Rovaniemi. As I drove around, there was in the Arctic circle tourist trap direction a pocket of ice fog so I headed there. From the side mirrors I saw good parhelia appear and I headed to road Rekkatie, which is my usual location in this direction. From there the right side parhelion really blazed up and I looked further out for 44° parhelion and indeed saw some colored segment there against the distant forests. This is only a single image, but there is indeed something right where I saw it (arrow). I try to flesh it out better by stacking handheld dng images when I have time. Anyway, that would be my first 44° parhelion. The overexposed spot next to the spruce tree is 22° parhelion.

### 7/8 - 2025-01-06 17:51 UTC

But 20 minutes later, 12:40, I was already up there at road Rekkatie photographing the bright parhelion (4 January 2024 Rovaniemi). In the following are series of photos that show first a vertical pillar opposite to the sun – which must be the Mikkilä's soul – and also the source of the ice fog in the 12:00 image. It has a little cloud spawning from the airport, from airplane engines or maybe they had operated snow blower. Mikkilä's soul is seen in 12:20 - 12:50 images.

Anyway, as to the 44° parhelion candidate, I will try to get photos of some 46° type halo with that phone for comparison. Photographing stars for measurement probably wouldn't work with it. Anyway, when I returned back and drove through the waning ice fog of this display, I saw only parhelia, no 22° tangent arc or 22° halo reaching to the top. So my bet is measurement will show it is 44° parhelion.
