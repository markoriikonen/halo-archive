### 2/7 - 2024-12-16 18:14 UTC

14-15 November 2024 Rovaniemi. Maybe the most interesting stack of the night, from the birdwatching tower. Diffuse spots of lower 24° plate arcs are seen, yellow arrows. The red arrow points to what looks like lower 9° plate arc. But these are not your usual lower arcs from raypath 3-25 and 3-26, respectively. Because the lamp is low, some 5 degrees below horizon, these are from 23-5 and 23-6. The lower 9 and 24° plate arcs B, if you will. Lower 24° B has been photographed already a handful of times, but the situation for the lower 9° B is unclear, the only candidate observation is this: https://t.co/ZfdKVlj3SC

### 3/7 - 2024-12-21 12:02 UTC

14-15 November 2024 Rovaniemi. A lunar view that I took in between the spotlight. 17 x 30s. I need to keep my other blocker along so I can simultaneously do lunar and spotlight. Or two spotlight.

Anyway, lower 9° plate arc is there so probably the brightening in the spotlight images is also lower 9° plate arc (but of type B). Then there is something above the 9° halo. If it is real, the only halo that fits the bill is the arc from rotating Moilanen crystals, the "12° tangent arc" or "Mikkilä arc". Would be my first, but I'll wait for a clearer case.

### 4/7 - 2024-12-21 12:49 UTC

14-15 November 2024 Rovaniemi. I had started 6 pm and returned to apartment around 6 am to realize that one of my lenses had no cap. So I drove back to the birdtower to look around. Nothing was found but as I turned around on the river ice to return to the car, in both car headlight pillar there was a brightening – Moilanen arc. So I took once more the equipment out, lamp to the birdtower and camera below on the river ice. Around thirty 30s exposures are stacked. I was thinking Moilanen helic arc might materialize against the relatively dark forest but no. There is some spike to the left, but I don't think the angle fits. Even if it would, I couldn't take it seriously.

Yeah, it was a long night. Sky was already getting light at the south horizon while photographing this Moilanen arc so there was nothing else to do but drive back to the apartment.

I regret of not finding this bird tower earlier. Definitely the best place to do spotlight in Rovaniemi. So easy to have both negative and positive lamp. If ice fog will form in that place again this winter, I might have lamp and camera placed both on the ice and tower to be able to quickly switch from negative to positive and vice versa if needed.
