### 1/12 - 2024-04-07 11:14 UTC

Winter's biggest night, 10/11 November 2023, Salmijärvi, Rovaniemi. I have already published snippets elsewhere (Taivaanvahti https://t.co/gx4mlN7Yco, The Halo Vault https://t.co/5BZGjV0qdh and on X https://t.co/OUHVCr2MlP), but now it is time to stack everything and have a look at the crystals as well. This was ice fog born of water fog. Didn't have thermometer, when the action got going around 2 am it was -6 C at the railway station and when I took the last photos around 5 am -10 C. No difference to Apukka because of the foggy conditions.

In this thread are the two swarms and their crystals at the east end of lake Salmijärvi (the action that followed at the Toramo pit will be its own post). After the first swarm the breeze shifted and things died down but I decided to wait. That was right move for after about 50 minutes the action came back with gusto.

So, first, here the first swarm and its crystals.
