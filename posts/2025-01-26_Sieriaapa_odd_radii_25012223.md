### 1/7 - 2025-01-26 15:00 UTC

Here follows stacks of the odd radius display on the night of 22/23 January 2025 Rovaniemi. I was at Sieriaapa, which is the best location darkness wise. It was crappy sort of ice fog with serious glow near the lamp. So I took series of darker and lighter photos to not miss what maybe visible near the lamp and further out.

As to the visuals, I didn't realize this immediately as an odd radius display. I noticed first the lower 24° plate arcs and then also lower 9°. They were quite ghosty. On a less conscious level I had registered the angular appearance of the 22° halo – which is caused by the combo of upper and lower 24° plate arcs and parhelia – already before that, but it took for a while to catch on.

The identity of the display's helic arc is anyone's guess. Pyramid or not? As is well known, once in while helic arc appears in a display that has no Parry arc or odd radius plate arcs.

Except for the end before I packed up and went to Jokkavaara pits (where it was all crap), the configuration was the same, the lamp being 3-4 degrees below the horizon. Counterparhelia is touching from below the upper 24°, giving it an appearance that is different from the lower 24°.

Lamp is Olight Javelot Turbo. Temp was below -20 C.
