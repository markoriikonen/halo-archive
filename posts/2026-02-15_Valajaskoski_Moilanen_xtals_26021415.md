### 1/7 - 2026-02-15 15:12 UTC

14/15 Feb 2026, Rovaniemi. Another night, another Moilanen arc. Background glow was strong and in most stacks Moila got overexposed, but in this heavily underexposed 42 x 8s set it shows up well. Sampled also crystals. The location once again happened to be the Valajaskoski hydroelectric station parking lot. Clear skies, didn't seem to be any thin fog layer above the ground. Left at midnight, back 5 am. Temp around -15 C upon leaving, below -20 C when coming back (Apukka -27 C at 5 am).
