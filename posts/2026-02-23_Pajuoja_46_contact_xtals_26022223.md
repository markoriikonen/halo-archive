### 16/32 - 2026-02-28 11:31 UTC

22/23 Feb 2026, Rovaniemi. 1st stage crystals. Hasn't the Moilanen arc already mocked us enough? All these decades it's been glittering in front of our noses and we still don't know how it is formed. Moilanen arc will never be solved by taking more light microscope photos. The resolution is not sufficient. We need ESEM. In Fairbanks Moilanen arcs are abundant in the power plant plumes and there is also ESEM. Someone needs to apply for a grant for a winter in Fairbanks to take those ESEM photos. Barring an ingenuous theoretical breakthough on crystal growth, it's our best chance at cracking the Moilanen mystery.
