### 1/7 - 2026-02-20 01:09 UTC

19/20 Feb 2026, Rovaniemi. I have been wanting to have a "Sievi type" display this winter: https://t.co/YNowxWRSnN Now it came and it's good the lamp elevation differs from the usual near-horizon.

There is no Parry arc or tangent arc. It would seem like this is due to Parry-Lowitz orientation in thin plates, but the simulations I have managed leave much to hope for.

I think I have also a crystal sample involving this stage but I don't expect much of it. I was looking forward to photograph halos the whole night, but the fun was cut short by the snowgun stopping at around 2225. I was in a nearby field and could hear its sound change and then just silence. I stayed around and later a guy came who said aggregate had malfunctioned. Won't be happening anything for a couple of days.

Too bad I didn't check the time when the machine stopped. It's would be nice to know how long diamond dust lingers after the source is cut off. Now I have only an estimate. I checked the time at 2234 and estimated the gun had stopped 10 minutes earlier. The last of the pillar in the flood of the gunning site building light disappeared at 2300. So the diamond dust was around for 35 minutes after the machine stopped. Don't know of course if somewhere else it was still present. The weather was quite calm.

-25 C in car thermometer.
