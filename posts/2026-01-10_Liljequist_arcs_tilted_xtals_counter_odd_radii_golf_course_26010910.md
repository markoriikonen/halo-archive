### 14/21 - 2026-01-26 00:02 UTC

9/10 Jan 2026, Rovaniemi. I found more photos around the odd radius stage when browsing the memory card (lucky I had not formatted it yet) and made a longer stack of 11 frames (8172, 8182-8191). The gif here is flashing this stage with the preceding "normal" stage.

What strikes immediately from this comparison is that Liljequist arcs turn spot-like. If I had only this stack, not the earlier stage, I would have checked both the upper and lower 24° plate arcs and possibly gone wrong. I think we should be even more careful now with the identification of the "ten and two o'clock spots" spots which are not outright clear 24° spots, particularly in displays that have Lowitz / Liljequist arcs. There have accumulated quite many of these mysterious spots over the years. One is the display by Martikainen on 6 February 2021 in Juva: https://t.co/OHjax3YOwW Another one I can remember right off the bat is the later stages of the Jutland display: https://t.co/Vckjx7dj8p

But that's not all. Above the treeline is seen a more profound change in the concave Liljequist arcs as they become tilted outwards. Something also appears outside the counterparhelia, maybe just a continuation of these tilted arcs, maybe a separate feature.

There is familiarity to this stuff. We have had similar kind of scenes in earlier displays: https://t.co/nhNBZKHivK
