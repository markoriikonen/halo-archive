### 1/1 - 2025-01-11 10:49 UTC

Sometimes it may not be quite clear whether the fog you are in is ice fog or water fog and so you want to check for fogbow. But you don't need to put the light torch on your car roof and walk into the beam. Just open the car door and point the beam in the air: the fogbow – if it is water fog – will show just fine while holding the lamp in your hand.

In this video, that I took the other night at the Ounasvaara ski center, I have the lamp in my hand all the time. I could get more of the fogbow circumference by increasing the distance between camera and lamp, i.e. extending the lamp hand as far behind my back and camera hand as far on front of me as possible.

This was not 100% water fog, some crystals were in the mix.
