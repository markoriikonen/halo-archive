### 1/5 - 2024-02-08 15:07 UTC

Same place same display. I went today, 8 February, to where I photographed the last hours of the last night (the video in the previous post). As shown by the single photo here, this is very much the same display, nothing had changed (also temp was unchanged -25 C).

In the beginning the display was caused by Suosiola plume, this is revealed by the decorated crystals. As I photographed the crystals, little by little the dish accumulated clean, thin plates. Those are snow gun crystals. The snow gun plume was entering the scene, I could see its uniform fog coming closer from north and then engulf the place. This resulted in parhelia losing their edge. The distinct spot on the side largely disappeared as intensity spread vertically. Smoke stack plume trumped the snow gun plume!

After I had finished I drove to Anttilanvaara to check that Suosiola plume was really coming to where I was. That was confirmed. Notice the snow gun plume further behind.

All right, soon it is again dark. I liked the balmy conditions of last night, this one will be colder. I wonder when I can sleep. Got just one hour in the morning. Probably should not write these posts.
