### 1/1 - 2024-04-22 06:53 UTC

I am publishing here an old video of a self-made ice plate (29 February 2020, Tampere) because it is important reference for some features in the puddle plates. This plate was grown by letting water freeze on a plastic tray over the night. This particular night was humid enough for the upper surface to acquire crystal growth which makes the halos seen in the video. Earlier I had put this video on Youtube but the site compressed it pretty much useless, let's see it if comes out any better here. The original file was available for download in my Google drive, but approaching storage limit forced me to remove all long videos.

It is quite wasteful to leave the crystal growth on the plate's surface at the mercy of nature, on most winter nights you won't have the right conditions. Much better to device some kind of a set-up in which you place the frozen plate as a lid over an identically shaped bowl, inside which is a little heated water reservoir to provide moisture for crystal growth on the plate's down facing side.
