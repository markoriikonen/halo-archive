### 2/4 - 2025-11-22 10:39 UTC

20/21 Nov 2025 Rovaniemi. In the next set (14 x 30s) I focused on the nadir arc, which nicely glittered against the ground at my feet. Although this halo is no-brainer, not much photos exists of it. That's because the Finnish tradition has been to do the "sky flipping trick" when light is below horizon, meaning the arc was regarded by the chasers just as zenith arc. Thus not much interest has been been spared on photographing its glitter low down against the restless ground. But the Finnish approach – one of whose chief purveyors I have been – is wrong. No sky-flipping should be performed, as Tape has already argued in the past. The arc in this image is as independent halo in its own right as the zenith arc is.
