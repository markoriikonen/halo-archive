### 1/1 - 2026-03-30 19:54 UTC

Indeed, pink and green-cyan are the dominant colors in psychedelic glitter with some other colors in lesser numbers. They are from thin film interference. This phenomenon in ice crystals has been known before halo people paid attention to it. There is a 2002 paper by Foster and Hallett, "The alignment of ice crystals in changing electric fields", where they describe colored glitter in a cloud chamber experiment (in chapter "Thin film interference colours and crystal thickness") and provide also one photo. You may read also my short post "Psychedelic colors in diamond dust" https://t.co/TFXa265eU1 and the comment by Nicolas Lefaudeux.
