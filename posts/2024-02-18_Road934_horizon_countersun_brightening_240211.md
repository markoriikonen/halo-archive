### 1/1 - 2024-02-18 19:12 UTC

Driving towards Rovaniemi from Levi direction, this pillar brightening at the horizon, on 11 February 2024, was almost blindingly bright. But I was slow to react and further down the road trees were blocking the view. So I turned around at the first chance and came back to take that photo because this got me thinking about the "pillar cza" and "integrated cza" stuff. See Jia Hao's post in The Halo Vault for reference: https://t.co/Vckjx7cLiR

Also: https://t.co/erTh2E3HXr

Seeing this intense brightening converted me instantly to a true believer of the idea by Lefaudeux that pillar at the horizon can act as a light source for circumzenith arc. But strangely, it looks like we are still on a lookout for this "pillar cza" as all three known cases (Levi, Siziwang Qi and Jutland) seem to be of the "integrated cza" type. True, the Levi display photos published in Taivaanvahti (https://t.co/XA5E30E8hG) have an arc confined only under cza. But since then I have made from two raws a stack (unpublished) which shows it going in b-r well beyond 46° halo, being thus longer than the "pillar cza" of the simulations. You get the feeling that had the photographic coverage been wider, we would have seen it go all around the zenith.
