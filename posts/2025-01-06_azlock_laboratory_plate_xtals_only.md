### 1/5 - 2025-01-06 14:45 UTC

Here follows a motley of ice plate crystals that I have photographed in the recent few days. I don't know what I am seeing in some of these photos. Like what are those rectangular looking features? First I was thinking of prism faces of Parry oriented crystals, but now I am not so sure. In many photos the crystals are greatly eroded, maybe these are just eroded basal faces? I don't know if erosion is something that happens during photography (heat from the lamp, my warm exhalation which I try to direct elsewhere), or if it was already like that before I touched the plate. At least inside snow pack growth and sublimation are happening constantly, making crystals with proper faces on the one hand and rounded eaten crystals on the other.
