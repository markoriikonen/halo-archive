### 1/6 - 2024-02-21 20:37 UTC

Snow pack display on the night of 18/19 February, 45 frames flip stacked. Shown is just the b-r version because it is so much better than the color versions.

I wrote some words about this session in the beginning of previous post: https://t.co/Zyk5XXoSfW

I didn't see 9° halo visually or from camera screen and so thought this won't come out as good as the previous ones, but I see my worry was unnecessary. True, 9 and 24° halos are diminutive this time, but they have just given the stage for 23° halo which could not be discerned in earlier displays.

18, 20, 22 and 23° halos have intensity variations that tells the crystals have come down in rudimentary plate orientations. There were hints of this already in the earlier displays. Oriented crystals is not really the impression I get when looking at those samples that I have taken during the short moment that the thrown snow comes down. Wonder if my sampling process is biased. Or maybe the right crystals are on the dish, but I photograph the wrong ones.

This was the surface layer. I will thread crystals and also the deeper layer display here once I get to it.
